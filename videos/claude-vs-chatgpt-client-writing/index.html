What the video covers

The video takes a typical freelance brief, a 1,200-word B2B blog post, and runs it through both tools with the same prompt and the same rounds of edits, then looks at how far each draft is from something a client could receive.

The pattern matches what published comparisons by working writers consistently find: Claude's first draft tends to need fewer structural changes and carry fewer filler sentences, while ChatGPT is quicker at the research-heavy parts and better at pulling in current examples. Both intros need a human rewrite.

If you only do this kind of work occasionally, either tool is fine. If it is your main income, the difference adds up over a month of pieces.
